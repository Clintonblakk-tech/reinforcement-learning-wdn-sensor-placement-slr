## PICOS framework

### Population

**Include:** Water distribution networks (WDNs), including municipal, urban and benchmark water-supply networks, in which sensor placement is investigated for leak detection, contamination detection, water-quality monitoring or related network-monitoring objectives.

**Exclude:** Studies focusing exclusively on non-water networks, including telecommunications, electricity, gas and transportation networks, without direct relevance to water distribution networks.

### Intervention

**Include:** Studies applying reinforcement-learning algorithms, including but not limited to Q-learning, Deep Q-Network (DQN), Double DQN, DDPG, PPO, A3C and other RL approaches, to sensor placement, sensor deployment or sensor-location optimisation in water distribution networks.

**Exclude:** Studies that do not apply reinforcement learning to sensor placement or sensor-location optimisation in water distribution networks.

### Comparison

**Include:** Comparisons with traditional optimisation and heuristic approaches, including genetic algorithms, particle swarm optimisation (PSO), simulated annealing and other relevant methods. Studies without an explicit comparator may also be included when they investigate RL-based sensor placement in WDNs and provide sufficient evidence of method performance.

**Exclude:** Studies whose comparisons are unrelated to sensor placement or do not provide evidence relevant to the review objectives.

### Outcomes

**Include:** Sensor-placement performance and optimisation outcomes, including leak-detection accuracy, contamination-detection performance, sensor coverage, detection time, sensor-installation cost, redundancy reduction, computational efficiency, network-monitoring effectiveness and solution quality.

**Exclude:** Studies reporting outcomes unrelated to sensor placement or network monitoring, such as pump scheduling or energy optimisation alone, without evaluating sensor-placement performance.

### Study design

**Include:** Original peer-reviewed journal articles and conference papers published between 2000 and 2026, written in English, that investigate reinforcement-learning-based sensor placement, sensor deployment or sensor-location optimisation in water distribution networks.

**Exclude:** Editorials, commentaries, book reviews, review articles, non-peer-reviewed publications and publications without sufficient methodological or technical information to assess their relevance.

## Additional eligibility conditions

**Additional inclusion condition:** Studies investigating RL-based sensor placement in WDNs, whether evaluated independently or compared against other RL algorithms, traditional optimisation methods or heuristic approaches.

**Additional exclusion condition:** Studies focusing exclusively on pump scheduling, energy optimisation, water-demand forecasting, hydraulic simulation or other unrelated water-distribution tasks without investigating sensor placement.
