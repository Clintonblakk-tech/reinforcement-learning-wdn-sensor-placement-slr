Review title
============
Reinforcement Learning for Optimal Sensor Placement in Water Distribution Networks:
A Systematic Literature Review

Review configuration and protocol
=================================
Covidence review settings identify the review type as "Systematic review", the question type as
"Other" (specified as "Intervention"), and the research area as "Engineering, maths, physics and
technology". The stated review purpose in Covidence is a Master's degree thesis/dissertation.

The protocol and eligibility criteria were established within Covidence using the PICOS
(Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome, Study Design) framework. The protocol was not
separately registered in an external public prospective review registry.

PICOS population
================
Water distribution networks, including municipal, urban and benchmark water-supply networks,
where sensor placement is investigated for leak detection, contamination detection, water-quality
monitoring or related network-monitoring objectives.

PICOS intervention
==================
Reinforcement-learning algorithms, including Q-learning, DQN, Double DQN, DDPG, PPO, A3C and
other RL approaches, applied to sensor placement, deployment or sensor-location optimisation
in water distribution networks.

PICOS comparison
================
Traditional optimisation and heuristic approaches, including genetic algorithms, particle swarm
optimisation, simulated annealing and other relevant methods. Studies without an explicit
comparator could also be included when they investigated RL-based sensor placement in WDNs
and provided sufficient evidence of method performance.

PICOS outcomes
==============
Sensor-placement performance and optimisation outcomes, including leak-detection accuracy,
contamination-detection performance, sensor coverage, detection time, sensor-installation cost,
redundancy reduction, computational efficiency, network-monitoring effectiveness and solution
quality.

PICOS study design
==================
Original peer-reviewed journal articles and conference papers published between 2000 and 2026
in English, investigating RL-based sensor placement, deployment or sensor-location optimisation
in water distribution networks.

Eligibility exclusions
======================
Excluded populations: studies focused exclusively on non-water networks without direct relevance
to water distribution networks.
Excluded interventions: studies not applying RL to sensor placement or sensor-location
optimisation in WDNs.
Excluded comparisons: comparisons unrelated to sensor placement or not providing evidence
relevant to the review objectives.
Excluded outcomes: studies reporting unrelated outcomes (for example, pump scheduling or energy
optimisation alone) without evaluating sensor-placement performance.
Excluded study designs: editorials, commentaries, book reviews, review articles, non-peer-reviewed
publications and publications lacking sufficient methodological or technical information.

Information sources
===================
Web of Science, Scopus, Google Scholar, ScienceDirect, Engineering Village, Springer Nature,
and IEEE Xplore.

Publication-year range
======================
2000–2026 across all seven sources (reviewer confirmation and Covidence eligibility criteria).

Selection process
=================
417 records imported; 40 duplicates identified; 377 title/abstract records screened; 336 excluded;
41 full texts assessed; 37 full-text exclusions; 4 studies included.

Screening and extraction
========================
Covidence was used to manage eligibility criteria, screening, full-text assessment and data
extraction. Covidence settings specify one reviewer for title/abstract screening, one for full-text
review and one for data extraction. Extraction 2 ("Mapping and describing the evidence") was
selected. The four included study records are individually marked as extraction completed in
Covidence. The aggregate Covidence dashboard displayed 3 In progress and 1 Complete; the reason
for this aggregate classification is not established by the available screenshots.

Search-date note
================
Covidence review settings show 12 November 2026 as the date of last search. Covidence import
history separately records source imports between 6 and 12 November 2026. Import dates are not
necessarily the exact database query execution dates.

Synthesis
=========
Narrative and comparative synthesis. No pooled effect estimate was calculated because the included
studies differed in algorithms, network representations, placement formulations, objectives and
evaluation measures.

Quality appraisal
=================
An adapted ten-criterion descriptive appraisal; not a validated standardised risk-of-bias instrument.
No overall quality score was used to rank or exclude studies.
