SCREENING AND EXTRACTION PROVENANCE

Covidence was used to configure the review eligibility criteria and manage reference screening,
full-text assessment and data extraction. The review settings specify one reviewer for title/abstract
screening, one for full-text assessment and one for data extraction.

The PICOS eligibility framework is documented in:
01_protocol_and_methods/Covidence_PICOS_Protocol_and_Eligibility.md

The four included study records are individually marked as extraction completed in the user-provided
Covidence screenshots. One record (#7, Sukya et al., Bayesian optimisation) also shows comparison
completed. The aggregate dashboard simultaneously displayed 3 In progress and 1 Complete. The
available screenshots do not explain this discrepancy; it must not be interpreted as three missing
extraction records.

The Covidence import history and reconciled PRISMA counts are provided in this folder. The complete
377-record title/abstract decision-level export has not been recovered. The 37 full-text exclusion
records and four-study extraction export have been recovered locally but are not yet included in this
GitHub repository.

Do not treat the manuscript-derived synthesis in 05_data_extraction as a substitute for the original
Covidence extraction export.
