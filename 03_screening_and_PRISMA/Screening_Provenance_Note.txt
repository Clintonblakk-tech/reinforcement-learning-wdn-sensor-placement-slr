SCREENING AND EXTRACTION PROVENANCE

Covidence was used to manage reference screening and data extraction. The review was conducted by one reviewer.

The Covidence import history and reconciled PRISMA counts are provided in this folder. Original title/abstract decisions, report-level full-text decisions, and completed extraction forms have not yet been exported into this repository. Their absence is recorded in 10_pending_author_exports/README_PENDING_EXPORTS.txt.

Do not treat the manuscript-derived synthesis in 05_data_extraction as a substitute for the original Covidence extraction records.
