# Author declarations — confirmation required

This file records outstanding author confirmations; it is not a final declaration.

## Required from the author team
- Final author names and order.
- Official affiliations for every author.
- Corresponding author and institutional email.
- Author-by-author CRediT contributions.
- Funding status and grant details, or confirmation of no specific funding.
- Competing-interest declaration from each author.
- Protocol registration status and identifier, or confirmation that no registration occurred.
- Confirmation of the ethics statement.
- Accurate disclosure of generative AI use in research/writing/figures.
- Approval from all authors for manuscript submission and public data release.
- Licence selection after confirming rights to all materials.

## Proposed data availability wording (only after deposit)
The review methods, database-specific search strategies, Covidence import history, PRISMA counts, included-study register, manuscript-derived synthesis, and appraisal materials are available at the project GitHub repository and the associated Zenodo record [DOI].

Do not claim original Covidence screening decisions, full-text exclusion records or completed extraction exports are public until those exact exports have been deposited.

## Journal note
Environmental Modelling & Software requires CRediT author contributions, competing-interest disclosure, funding disclosure and a data availability statement. Review-paper authors are advised to contact the Editor-in-Chief to discuss topic and coverage before submission.

## AI disclosure
Use an accurate author-approved statement describing the actual tools and tasks. Do not state that AI was not used if it was used. AI systems must not be listed as authors.
