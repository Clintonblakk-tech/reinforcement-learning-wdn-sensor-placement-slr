Adapted ten-criterion descriptive appraisal used in the review.

Criteria:
1. Research objective
2. Network characterisation
3. RL methodology
4. Sensor-placement formulation
5. Experimental configuration
6. Performance evaluation
7. Comparative evaluation
8. Statistical validation
9. Reproducibility
10. Reporting of limitations

Ratings:
Yes = adequately addressed.
Partially = addressed, but important information was missing.
No = not adequately addressed.
Unclear = insufficient information for a reliable judgement.

The framework was used descriptively. It is not presented as a validated standardised instrument.
No overall numerical quality score was used to rank or retrospectively exclude studies.

The current manuscript reports 19 Yes, 18 Partially and 3 No judgements across 40 criterion-level assessments.
These counts are descriptive and should not be interpreted as a validated overall quality score.
