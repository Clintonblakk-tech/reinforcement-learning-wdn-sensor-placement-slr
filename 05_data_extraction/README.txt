# Covidence Data Extraction: Records, Provenance and Status

## 1. Source export

The Covidence data extraction export was recovered on 15 November 2026.

Export selection: Data extraction consensus + individual data.  
Export format: Single sheet (CSV).  
Original source filename: `review_843067_20261115084219.csv`.

The original CSV is preserved in this repository as:

`Original_Covidence_Extraction_Export_2026-11-15.csv`

This file is retained as the source export and has not been intentionally reconstructed or manually supplemented.

## 2. Organised extraction workbook

The organised Excel workbook is available as:

`Covidence_Extraction_Register_Individual_and_Consensus_Records.xlsx`

It provides a structured presentation of the recovered extraction information, including individual and consensus records where available.

The workbook is an organised research record and should be distinguished from the original Covidence CSV export.

## 3. Supporting documentation

`Extraction_Export_Provenance_and_Limitations.txt` documents the export provenance, record status and known limitations.

`Evidence_Gap_Reconciliation.csv` records evidence gaps and separately documented reconciliations.

`Manuscript_Derived_Study_Synthesis.csv` contains the study synthesis prepared for the review.

## 4. Included studies

The extraction work concerns the four studies included in the systematic review:

- Ettyem et al. (2026), Covidence ID 286.
- Song et al. (2022), Covidence ID 18.
- Sukya et al. (2026), SPARC, Covidence ID 8.
- Sukya et al. (2026), Bayesian optimisation, Covidence ID 7.

## 5. Known limitations

The Ettyem et al. extraction record has a blank field for "Network or benchmark name".

The Sukya et al. Bayesian optimisation extraction record has a blank field for "Possible conflicts of interest for study authors".

These fields are not silently completed in the original source export. Any additional interpretation is documented separately.

Covidence screenshots showed all four individual study records marked "You completed extraction". The aggregate dashboard separately displayed three records as In progress and one as Complete. This discrepancy is retained as a workflow-verification note.

The complete decision-level export for all 377 title/abstract screening records was not recovered.

Screening and extraction were conducted by one reviewer.

## 6. Research transparency

The original CSV, organised workbook and provenance documentation are provided to support transparency and traceability. Users should consult the original export and its accompanying documentation when interpreting the organised records.
